# Affect → route map (RGL theory lock)

**Locked:** 2026-09-21 America/Chicago · Matt Brown / PsychArts  
**clinical_claim: false** · not DSM · not "AI has feelings"

Working theory for Path A/B residual routing: named affects are **labels on clusters of movement impulses**. Residual/salience lights which cluster is on. Machine routes are analogues (rhyme or inverse ok) — not emotion simulation.

## Science lineage (cite carefully)

Bridges open science on affect and action readiness discussed with **Lisa Feldman Barrett, Ph.D.** on Huberman Lab (*How to Understand Emotions*), and her broader program (e.g. *How Emotions Are Made*). Barrett’s work treats emotions as constructed multimodal summaries involving body, prediction, and concepts — including discussion of movement, sensation, prediction, and learning.

**RGL’s cluster list and impulse glosses below are PsychArts / Matt’s bridging language for residual routing.** Do not treat them as verbatim Barrett taxonomy or clinical protocol.

## What Path B has exercised

Proofs 4–15 mostly probed fight / flight / freeze-ish recipes (thrash, kite, HOLD, interrupt). Learn-arm (P11–P15) showed greedy route credit can overweight fight-like commits; outcome-gated / sleep / slow LR stop the dig but do not yet beat a good frozen thrash recipe.
